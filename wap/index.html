The whole thing

  1. Hooks: everyone twerks.
  2. Verses: four spots, wall, top, bottom, off. When the cue says switch, move to your next spot.
  3. Your path: wall, wall, top, bottom, off, then back to wall. You climb on someone, someone climbs on you, then you rest.
  4. Start and end in name order: Savi, Tim, Ali, Aryaman, Jishnuu turn around one at a time, and walk off one at a time.

Running it live

  • Hooks are the reset. Lost? Twerk until the next hook.
  • On a switch, just move to your next spot: wall, wall, top, bottom, off.
  • On OFF? Chill. Hype the others, slap the top guy if he's cool with it.
  • Savi calls switches if timing drifts.
  • Everyone lip-syncs every line.
  • Practice: 2 sessions of about 45 min. Walk the rotation slowly first, then with the music.
  • If the outro runs long, Jishnuu keeps going till it ends.
