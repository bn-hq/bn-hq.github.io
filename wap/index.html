Running it live

  • Two jobs only: shake it, or do nothing (walk, hover, slap, catch, hype, stand). Nobody has to actually dance.
  • Check the slaps and catches with each other at practice so nobody's surprised on stage.
  • Hooks are unison, everyone shaking. In the verses watch your own column.
  • On HYPE? Sell it. You're the crowd, and it makes whoever's twerking look ten times better.
  • Lost? ST until the next switch.
  • Everyone is BASE at least once, and everyone gets a solo.
  • Lost? Twerk till the next hook. Hooks are the reset point.
  • Savi calls switches if timing drifts.
  • Commit to the faces. Confident bad dancing beats embarrassed okay dancing.
  • Practice: 2 sessions of about 45 min. Drill the moves first, then run section by section.
  • The music video is the censored version. Perform to whichever track you practiced with.
